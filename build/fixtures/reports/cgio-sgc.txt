On the SGC schedule reviewed September 20, 2026, Standard starts at $50 per card up to a $1,500 declared value. The next Standard band is $85 up to $3,500. Expedited starts at $150 and rises by declared value to a $3,750 base fee above $100,000. Return shipping and other applicable charges are extra. What is the SGC turnaround time in 2026? On the SGC schedule reviewed September 20, 2026, Standard is listed at 40+ business days and Expedited at 2–3 business days. The clock begins after SGC unboxes and logs the order, and the published times are estimates rather than guarantees. Does SGC require a membership or minimum submission? SGC’s terms allow a customer to submit one or more items, and its public direct schedule does not list a membership fee. Check the live order flow because submission requirements and promotions can change. Does SGC charge an upcharge for expensive cards? SGC prices service by declared-value band. Its terms state that it may increase the base service fee when the submitted value does not correspond with fair market value, so use a defensible declared value and confirm the selected tier before checkout. How much is SGC return shipping? On the schedule reviewed August 8, 2026, domestic return shipping for 1–5 standard-size cards starts at $15 by USPS Priority for packages under $2,000, or $25 by FedEx Ground or USPS for packages under $10,000. Larger orders and higher-value packages cost more, and inbound shipping is separate. What is the SGC grading scale? SGC uses a numeric 1–10 scale with half-point grades through most of the range. Its two top designations are Pristine 10 and Gem Mint 10. The official scale describes the visible condition associated with each grade. Are SGC holders waterproof or UV-resistant? No. SGC’s terms state that its holders are not waterproof or UV-resistant. Store graded cards dry, away from prolonged direct sunlight, and in protective packaging appropriate for transit. Does SGC grade Pokémon and other trading cards? SGC’s terms cover trading cards broadly, and its population reports include Pokémon and Magic cards. Check the live submission catalog for a specific set, autograph type, fragile item, or unusual format before mailing it. Is SGC grading worth it? It depends on the exact card, expected grade range, comparable sales, and all-in submission cost. Compare recent sold prices for the raw card and relevant SGC grades, then subtract grading, shipping, supplies, insurance, and selling fees. A grade does not guarantee a profit. Is SGC a good card grading company? SGC may fit a card when its published scale, service timing, holder, and sold-market comparisons align with your goal. Review SGC’s current terms and prices, compare recent sales for the exact card and grade, and compare other grading companies before deciding. No company is the best choice for every card. Should I pre-screen a card before submitting to SGC? A pre-screen can help organize what to inspect before you submit, but it does not predict SGC’s grade, authenticate a card, or guarantee value. Check the raw card carefully and compare relevant recent sales before deciding.
