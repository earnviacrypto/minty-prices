# Minty TCG Portfolio Tracker – Privacy Policy

Effective: November 22, 2026 (second version)

Minty TCG Portfolio Tracker ("Minty") is a free app for keeping track of trading cards and sealed products. This policy explains what information Minty handles and what it does with it.

## The short version

- Minty has no accounts and no sign-in.
- Your collection and your purchase prices stay on your own device, unless you turn on sync between your own devices.
- Minty has no analytics and no tracking, and sells nothing about you.

## What stays on your device

Everything you enter in Minty: the items in your collection, what you paid, sales, notes, your watchlist and your settings. It is saved in Minty's folder on your device and is never uploaded by Minty, unless you turn on sync (below). You can export it or delete it at any time; removing Minty and its folder removes it completely.

## What Minty downloads

To show prices and pictures, Minty downloads public information from:

- Minty's own shared price copy, kept with a hosting provider: daily market prices, price history, market indexes, graded prices, eBay deals and eBay listings.
- Free public card databases and price lists.
- Card and product pictures, from the sites that publish them.

Like any website, these services can see your device's internet (IP) address when Minty downloads from them. Minty sends them nothing about you or your collection – only the name or number of the card or product being looked up.

## Sync between your devices (only if you turn it on)

Sync is off until you set it up. It keeps the same portfolio on your own devices – for example your home computer, your work computer and your phone. If you set it up:

- What is shared: the items in your collection, what you paid, sales, notes, your wishlist and watchlist, folders, your list of games, release alerts, badges, and a few settings (currency, eBay site, alerts). Prices and pictures are not shared – each device gets its own – and neither are each device's own settings, such as the theme.
- How it travels: Minty locks your portfolio on your device with a key that only your devices have, then keeps one locked copy on Minty's sync service (run with a hosting provider) so your other devices can pick up changes. The sync service cannot read it, and neither can Minty's maker.
- What the sync service keeps: the locked copy; for each of your devices, a random device number and a scrambled (hashed) form of that device's own pass; and, for up to 10 minutes, a locked pairing message for a new device you are adding. Each device's name (such as your computer's name) travels inside the locked copy, so only your devices can read it.
- Your internet address: like any web service, the sync service sees your device's internet (IP) address when your device connects. To stop people guessing codes, it keeps the addresses of wrong code attempts for up to one hour.
- Removing a device: if you remove a device (for example one that was lost or stolen), the sync service refuses it from then on. It keeps a small locked note for that device, saying which of your devices removed it, for up to 60 days, then deletes it.
- Stopping: "Stop syncing" takes that device out. When the last of your devices stops syncing, the locked copy is deleted from the sync service. Nothing is deleted from your devices – each one keeps everything it has.

## Phone alerts (only if you turn them on)

Phone alerts are off until you set them up. If you turn them on, Minty sends each alert's text – for example a card's name and its new price – to the free ntfy service (ntfy.sh, or the server you choose), which passes it to the ntfy app on your phone. Anyone who knows your topic name can read those alerts, so pick a name nobody would guess. Nothing else from your collection is sent. Turning phone alerts off stops it.

## eBay listings and deals

Minty shows public eBay listings, such as "Check out these listings" on card pages and the Deals page. Minty does not collect, store or show personal information of eBay members – for example, seller usernames are not kept.

When you click an eBay link, it opens eBay's website in your browser. From then on, eBay's own privacy notice applies. The links carry Minty's eBay Partner Network tag, so eBay can tell the visit came from Minty and may pay Minty a commission if you buy something. Minty receives no personal information from eBay about you.

## Children

Minty is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Minty will ask you to review the change the next time you open it.
