# Minty TCG Portfolio Tracker – Privacy Policy

Effective: November 21, 2026 (adds phone alerts and pictures)

Minty TCG Portfolio Tracker ("Minty") is a free desktop app for keeping track of trading cards and sealed products. This policy explains what information Minty handles and what it does with it.

## The short version

- Minty has no accounts and no sign-in.
- Your collection, your purchase prices and your photos stay on your own computer.
- Minty has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and sells nothing about you.

## What stays on your computer

Everything you enter in Minty: the items in your collection, what you paid, sales, notes, your own photos, your watchlist and your settings. It is saved in Minty's folder on your computer and is never uploaded by Minty. You can export it or delete it at any time; removing Minty and its folder removes it completely.

## What Minty downloads

To show prices and pictures, Minty downloads public information from:

- Minty's shared price copy on GitHub (earnviacrypto/minty-prices): daily market prices, price history, market indexes, graded prices, eBay deals and eBay listings.
- Free public card databases and price lists.
- Card and product pictures, from the sites that publish them.

Like any website, these services can see your computer's internet (IP) address when Minty downloads from them. Minty sends them nothing about you or your collection – only the name or number of the card or product being looked up.

## Phone alerts (only if you turn them on)

Phone alerts are off until you set them up. If you turn them on, Minty sends each alert's text – for example a card's name and its new price – to the free ntfy service (ntfy.sh, or the server you choose), which passes it to the ntfy app on your phone. Anyone who knows your topic name can read those alerts, so pick a name nobody would guess. Nothing else from your collection is sent. Turning phone alerts off stops it.

## eBay listings and deals

Minty shows public eBay listings, such as "Check out these listings" on card pages and the Deals page. Minty's price copy reads them from eBay with eBay's official API and refreshes them at least every 6 hours. Each listing shows how long ago it was checked, and listings older than 6 hours are removed. Minty does not collect, store or show personal information of eBay members – for example, seller usernames are not kept.

When you click an eBay link, it opens eBay's website in your browser. From then on, eBay's own privacy notice applies. The links carry Minty's eBay Partner Network tag, so eBay can tell the visit came from Minty and may pay Minty a commission if you buy something. Minty receives no personal information from eBay about you.

## Children

Minty is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Minty will ask you to review the change the next time you open it.
